=====================================================================
                 CAFEANA PROJECT GUIDE - WHAT IS WHAT
=====================================================================

Cafeana is a full cafe ordering and management system made of 3 parts:

  1. A Flutter app (mobile + web)  -> customers order, staff manage the cafe
  2. Firebase (cloud backend)      -> login, database, push notifications
  3. ESP32 kitchen terminal        -> small device in the kitchen with an
                                      OLED screen + buzzer that alerts staff
                                      when a new order arrives

HOW AN ORDER FLOWS
  Customer places order (app)
     -> saved in Firestore "orders" collection with status "queued"
     -> Staff dashboard sees it instantly + ESP32 buzzes in the kitchen
     -> Staff taps "Start Preparing" -> status "preparing"
     -> Staff taps "Mark as Ready"   -> status "ready" (customer is notified)
     -> Staff taps "Complete"        -> status "completed" (goes to analytics)

USER ROLES (stored in Firestore users/{uid}.role)
  customer -> customer app (/customer/...)
  owner    -> full staff dashboard (every page)
  cashier  -> staff dashboard (Home, Queue, Feed, Menu, Inventory)
  kitchen  -> order queue only (no side navigation)

Q: If I download this project as a ZIP on another laptop, do the
   packages and dependencies still come with it?
A: No, and that is completely normal and intentional!
   In modern Flutter, Dart, and Node.js projects, heavy build and
   dependency folders (such as "node_modules", ".dart_tool", "build/",
   and ".venv/") are excluded from Git and ZIP downloads to keep the
   project lightweight and avoid system conflicts across different OSes.

   When you open the project on another laptop:
     1. Make sure Flutter SDK and Git are installed.
     2. Open your terminal in the "mobile_web_app" folder.
     3. Run:
          flutter pub get
        Flutter will read "pubspec.yaml" and automatically download
        every single package and library needed within seconds!
     4. (Optional) If you also want to use the background push
        notification dispatcher on that laptop, open "mobile_web_app/functions"
        and run:
          npm install

Q: How do I test the analytics if I just installed a fresh database?
A: You can seed realistic order data using our included REST seeder:
     1. Open the project root in your terminal.
     2. Run:
          node seed_mock_orders.js
     This automatically creates 60 days of historical completed orders
     (10-15 drinks/day, totaling ~₱100,000 across 538 orders) with
     realistic customer buying patterns, rush hours, and weekend surges.


=====================================================================
  ANALYTICS & MACHINE LEARNING SYSTEM GUIDE
=====================================================================

The Cafeana Analytics Suite is an end-to-end operational intelligence
engine built specifically for specialty cafes. It is divided into four
analytical pillars:

  1. Descriptive Analytics : What happened? (Historical sales, KPIs)
  2. Predictive Analytics  : What will happen? (AI time-series forecasts)
  3. Operational Simulation: How will operations flow? (Queues & Cash)
  4. Prescriptive Analytics: What should staff do? (Actionable advice)

All calculations live inside:
  mobile_web_app/lib/core/analytics/analytics_engine.dart
(2,386 lines of pure Dart code with zero external UI dependencies).
Because it is pure Dart, the exact same mathematical logic feeds both
the live on-screen charts and the downloadable Excel (.xlsx) workbooks!


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TRAINING WINDOW LOGIC
---------------------------------------------------------------------

* Time Filtering:
  The owner can select any preset time window:
  Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, This Week, This Month, Last 30 Days,
  Last 90 Days, This Year, All Time, or a Custom Date Range.

* Model Training Window:
  Machine learning time-series models need sufficient historical data to
  uncover cyclical weekly patterns.
    - Minimum training days required (kMinTrainingDays): 14 days
    - Fallback training days (kFallbackTrainingDays): 28 days
    - Maximum training days (kMaxTrainingDays): 365 days
  If the owner selects a short window (e.g., "Today" or "Yesterday"),
  descriptive stats show only today, while the predictive ML engine
  automatically widens its training window to the preceding 28 days
  ending on that date. This prevents model underfitting.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. DESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS ENGINE (WHAT HAPPENED)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Descriptive analytics aggregate raw completed order documents from
Firestore into meaningful cafe business metrics:

* Gross Revenue:
  The total peso sum of all valid, non-cancelled orders:
    Gross Revenue = Sum(order.total)
  where order.total represents the final transaction value.

* Net Deductions & Promo Savings:
  Calculates the difference between full retail menu price and the
  actual amount paid after promotional voucher codes.

* Average Order Value (AOV):
    AOV = Gross Revenue / Completed Orders
  Measures the average spending per customer visit (typically ₱180-₱220).

* Average Items per Order:
    Items / Order = Total Drink Units Sold / Total Orders
  Helps managers see if customers are ordering single drinks or
  purchasing rounds for groups.

* 24-Hour Peak Distribution:
  Categorizes orders into 24 one-hour bins (00:00 to 23:00).
  Identifies key operational shifts:
    - Morning Caffeine Rush: 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM
    - Afternoon Study / Chill Spike: 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM
    - Evening Wind-down: 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM

* 7-Day Weekday Performance:
  Breaks down sales by day of the week (Monday through Sunday):
    - Weekday volume vs. Weekend volume
    - Weekday Average Order Value (AOV per day)
  Reveals customer behavioral shifts (e.g., single espresso shots on
  Monday mornings vs. large sweet milk teas and pastries on Saturday).

* Category Revenue Contribution:
  Calculates the percentage share of gross sales generated by each
  beverage category:
    Share (%) = (Category Gross Sales / Store Gross Sales) * 100
  Highlights revenue drivers (e.g., Espresso vs. Cold Brew vs. Pastries).

* Boston Menu Engineering Ranking:
  Sorts all menu items by volume, gross revenue, and profitability.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. PREDICTIVE MACHINE LEARNING ENGINE (WHAT WILL HAPPEN NEXT)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Cafeana projects 14 days into the future (kForecastHorizonDays = 14)
using a Multi-Model Ensemble that blends three distinct algorithms.
By combining three models, the engine cancels out individual model bias
and delivers stable, trustworthy predictions.

── MODEL 1: Holt-Winters Triple Exponential Smoothing ────────────────
Best for: Capturing weekly cyclical waves (Saturday/Sunday surges).

* Mathematical Concept:
  Decomposes historical sales into three components:
    1. Level (L_t)     : The baseline sales volume
    2. Trend (T_t)     : The upward or downward growth rate
    3. Seasonality (S_t): 7-day cyclical weekly multiplier (L = 7)

* Update Equations:
    L_t = alpha * (Y_t / S_{t-7}) + (1 - alpha) * (L_{t-1} + T_{t-1})
    T_t = beta  * (L_t - L_{t-1}) + (1 - beta)  * T_{t-1}
    S_t = gamma * (Y_t / L_t)     + (1 - gamma) * S_{t-7}

* Grid Search Optimization:
  The engine runs an automated grid search across candidate parameters:
    alpha in [0.10, 0.20, 0.35]  (level responsiveness)
    beta  in [0.05, 0.10]        (trend responsiveness)
    gamma in [0.10, 0.25]        (seasonality responsiveness)
  It simulates past predictions and picks the exact parameter combination
  that minimizes the Sum of Squared Errors (SSE).

── MODEL 2: Ridge Autoregressive Model (AR-7 with L2 Regularization) ──
Best for: Capturing short-term momentum and moving-average stability.

* Mathematical Concept:
  Frames time-series prediction as a multi-feature regression model:
    Y_hat = w0 + w1*(t/100) + w2*sin(w) + w3*cos(w) + w4*Lag1 + w5*Lag7 + w6*MA7

* Features in the Vector:
    1. Intercept (w0) : Base volume level
    2. Time Index (w1): Normalized linear drift over time
    3. Sine & Cosine  : Harmonic Fourier terms mapping 7-day day-of-week
    4. Lag-1 (w4)     : Sales from yesterday (immediate momentum)
    5. Lag-7 (w5)     : Sales from the same day last week (weekly memory)
    6. MA-7 (w6)      : Rolling 7-day moving average (underlying trend)

* L2 Regularization (Ridge Penalty):
  Solves the normal equation with a shrinkage penalty lambda = 1.0:
    w = (X^T * X + lambda * I)^(-1) * (X^T * Y)
  This penalty prevents the model from overfitting or swinging wildly
  when historical order counts are small or noisy.

── MODEL 3: Seasonal Linear Trend Model ──────────────────────────────
Best for: Establishing a grounded long-term trajectory.

* Mathematical Concept:
  1. Computes empirical weekday weights (Monday-Sunday seasonal indices).
  2. De-seasonalizes the raw data: Y_clean = Y_t / Seasonal[weekday].
  3. Fits an Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) linear slope:
       Y_clean = Intercept + Slope * t
  4. Multiplies the forecast by the target day's weekday index.
  Acts as an objective baseline that prevents exponential runaway.

── DYNAMIC ENSEMBLE BLENDING (Inverse-RMSE Weighting) ────────────────
Instead of arbitrarily picking one model, Cafeana dynamically evaluates
how well each model performed on historical backtesting:

  1. Computes Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) for each model:
       RMSE = sqrt( (1/N) * Sum( (Y_actual - Y_fitted)^2 ) )

  2. Calculates inverse-error weights:
       w_HW    = (1 / RMSE_HW)    / Sum(1 / RMSE_all)
       w_Ridge = (1 / RMSE_Ridge) / Sum(1 / RMSE_all)
       w_SL    = (1 / RMSE_SL)    / Sum(1 / RMSE_all)

  3. Blends their 14-day forward predictions:
       Y_ensemble = w_HW * Y_HW + w_Ridge * Y_Ridge + w_SL * Y_SL

If one model experiences high error on a recent pattern, its weight
automatically shrinks, letting the more accurate models lead.

── FORECAST ACCURACY METRIC: (1 - WAPE) ──────────────────────────────
Traditional MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) divides by actual sales:
  |Y - Y_hat| / Y
On quiet days with 0 or 1 orders, MAPE divides by near-zero and distorts
accuracy. Cafeana uses Weighted Absolute Percentage Error (WAPE):

    WAPE = Sum( |Y_actual - Y_predicted| ) / Sum( Y_actual )
    Model Accuracy = (1 - WAPE) * 100%

A model with 88% accuracy means total prediction deviation across the
entire validation window was only 12%.

── DYNAMIC UNCERTAINTY BANDS (P10 Downside vs. P90 Upside) ───────────
Every forecast includes dynamic confidence envelopes based on residual
standard error (resStd):

* 14-Day Horizon Scaling:
  Uncertainty naturally increases the further we look into the future:
    Band Margin = 1.28 * resStd * sqrt( 1.0 + 0.04 * dayStep )

* P10 Forecast ("Rainy Day" / Downside Scenario):
    P10 = max(0, Forecast - Band Margin)
  There is a 90% statistical probability that sales will exceed this
  floor. Use this number for worst-case cash budgeting.

* P90 Forecast ("Rush Surge" / Upside Scenario):
    P90 = Forecast + Band Margin
  Represents peak weekend or event demand. Use this to ensure the cafe
  never runs out of milk, ice, or cups during unexpected surges.

── DIFFERENTIATED FORECAST VISUALIZATIONS ────────────────────────────
On the Analytics screen, the two predictive charts serve distinct roles:

  * Chart 1: 14-Day Cumulative Revenue Pace
    Plots the running cumulative total of predicted sales against the
    previous 14 days' actual running total.
    Tells the owner: "Are we pacing ahead or behind the past two weeks?"

  * Chart 2: 14-Day Daily Order Volume
    Plots the daily predicted drink count with shaded P10-P90 confidence
    bands.
    Tells the baristas: "How many drinks should we expect to brew each day?"


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. OPERATIONAL SIMULATIONS (QUEUES & CASH FLOW)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

── CUSTOMER QUEUE FLOW SIMULATION ───────────────────────────────────
Predicts hourly customer congestion for tomorrow using queueing theory:

* Parameters:
    - Hourly Arrival Rate (lambda): Expected orders arriving that hour
    - Service Rate (mu)          : 12 orders / barista / hour
    - Barista Capacity (C)       : Active Baristas * 12
    - Traffic Intensity (rho)    : lambda / C

* Wait Time Simulation:
    - Quiet    (lambda = 0) : 0 min wait
    - Optimal  (rho < 0.60) : 2 - 3 min wait
    - Steady   (rho < 0.85) : 4 - 7 min wait
    - Rush     (rho < 1.00) : 8 - 14 min wait (peak queue)
    - Congested(rho >= 1.00): 15 - 30+ min wait (queue overflow)

* Recommended Staffing:
  Automatically recommends the exact number of active baristas needed
  for each hour of the day so wait times stay under 5 minutes.

── 14-DAY CASH FLOW SCENARIOS ───────────────────────────────────────
Aggregates daily revenue predictions into executive financial insights:
  - Expected 14-Day Revenue: Baseline target
  - P10 Downside Safety Net: Minimum guaranteed cash floor
  - P90 Upside Potential   : Ceiling under high customer turnout
  - Daily Run Rate         : Expected 14-Day Revenue / 14
  - Weekend Surge Rate (%) : Percentage increase of Saturday/Sunday sales
                             compared to standard Monday-Friday averages.

── 6-MONTH ROLLING FINANCIAL PROJECTION ─────────────────────────────
Projects store revenue across a half-year horizon:
  - 5 Historical Months: Verified actual earnings
  - Current Month      : Actual sales to date + projected sales for
                         remaining days in the month
  - Next Month         : Forward ML forecast based on seasonal trends


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. PRESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS ENGINE (WHAT STAFF SHOULD DO)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Prescriptive analytics translates raw numbers into specific daily tasks:

── INVENTORY RESTOCK OPTIMIZATION ───────────────────────────────────
Prevents both stockouts (running out of milk) and waste (spoilage).

* Calculation:
  1. Evaluates past sales velocity for each menu item.
  2. Maps menu items to required ingredients via product recipes.
  3. Forecasts 7-day future ingredient demand:
       7-Day Demand = Daily Rate * Growth Factor * 7 days
  4. Applies a 1.2x - 1.3x Safety Stock buffer:
       Target Stock = ceil( 7-Day Demand * 1.3 )
  5. Computes exact reorder quantity:
       Reorder Units = max(0, Target Stock - Current Stock)

* Restock Status Flags:
  - Critical : Stock is at 0 or will run out in < 24 hours (Reorder NOW)
  - Low Stock: Stock will run out within 1 to 3 days
  - Healthy  : Adequate stock cover for the upcoming 7 days

── PRODUCT MOMENTUM TRACKING ────────────────────────────────────────
Splits the selected time window in half (First Half vs. Second Half)
and measures the sales velocity change:

    Growth (%) = (Second Half Units - First Half Units) / First Half Units

  * Rising Stars  (Growth > +15%):
    Beverages rapidly gaining traction among customers.
    Action: Feature prominently on the landing page and social media.

  * Declining Items (Growth < -15%):
    Beverages losing popularity.
    Action: Review taste profile, adjust pricing, or bundle with snacks.

── BOSTON MENU ENGINEERING MATRIX ───────────────────────────────────
Categorizes every menu item into four quadrants based on:
  - Popularity Threshold   : 70% of average item sales volume
  - Profitability Threshold: Store-wide average price per unit

  1. STARS (High Volume, High Price):
     Your best-sellers and profit drivers.
     Action: Maintain high quality and recipe consistency.

  2. PLOWHORSES (High Volume, Low Price):
     Popular customer staples that yield smaller profit margins.
     Action: Consider a slight price adjustment (+₱5-₱10) or pair
     with premium add-ons (extra espresso shot, syrup pumps).

  3. PUZZLES (Low Volume, High Price):
     High-margin gourmet drinks that don't sell frequently enough.
     Action: Reposition on menu boards; instruct baristas to verbally
     recommend them.

  4. DOGS (Low Volume, Low Price):
     Low demand and low profitability.
     Action: Consider replacing them with seasonal specials.

── CUSTOMER RFM SEGMENTATION & CHURN PREVENTATIVE FLOW ──────────────
Analyzes customer purchasing history along two core behavioral axes:
  - Recency (R)  : Days elapsed since their most recent order
  - Frequency (F): Total number of orders placed in store history

Customers are sorted into five strategic cohorts:

  1. Champions (VIP Regulars):
     Frequency >= 4 orders AND Recency <= 7 days.
     The cafe's most passionate advocates.
     Action: Offer secret menu tastings and personalized perks.

  2. Loyal Patrons:
     Frequency >= 3 orders AND Recency <= 14 days.
     Steady weekly regulars.
     Action: Loyalty rewards and milestone discounts.

  3. Promising High-Value:
     Frequency <= 2 orders, Recency <= 10 days, Spend >= ₱300.
     New customers who placed large, expensive orders.
     Action: Welcome-back voucher to convert them into regulars.

  4. At-Risk (Churn Warning):
     Frequency >= 2 orders, but Recency between 15 and 30 days.
     Past regulars whose visit pattern has suddenly stopped.
     Action: Send automated re-engagement offer: "We miss you! 20% off".

  5. Occasional / Inactive:
     Recency > 30 days or one-time walk-ins.
     Action: Promote new seasonal beverage launches.

* Customer Retention Rate:
    Retention Rate (%) = (Repeat Customers / Total Customers) * 100
  Tracks how successfully the cafe turns first-time visitors into repeat
  regular customers.

── SMART PRODUCT BUNDLING (Market Basket Analysis) ──────────────────
Scans all multi-item orders in the database to identify products
frequently purchased together in the same basket (e.g., Iced Caramel
Macchiato + Almond Croissant).

  - Calculates co-occurrence frequency across all multi-item receipts.
  - Automatically suggests combo deals with an attractive 10% discount:
      Combo Price = (Price A + Price B) * 0.90
  - Encourages customers to add food to beverage orders, raising AOV.
