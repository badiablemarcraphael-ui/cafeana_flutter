STEP 5: UPLOAD YOUR `.aab` & ROLL OUT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On the left menu, go to "Testing -> Closed testing" (or "Production").
   - Note: For personal developer accounts created after Nov 2023, Google requires running
     a Closed Test with 12 testers opted in for 14 days before unlocking Production.
2. Click "Create new release".
3. Under "App integrity / Play App Signing", let Google manage your signing key (default).
4. Upload `mobile_web_app/build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`.
5. Release notes:
   <en-US>
   Initial release of Caféana? featuring online drink customization, real-time order status tracking, IoT kitchen queue sync, and smart café analytics.
   </en-US>
6. Click "Next" -> "Save" -> "Start rollout".
